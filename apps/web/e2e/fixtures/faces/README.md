# Face fixtures

Official portraits of US officials from Wikimedia Commons. As works of the US federal
government they are in the public domain. Each person has two different portraits, plus a
mirrored, cropped copy of the first, scaled down to 480 pixels.

- `obama-1`, `obama-3`: File:President_Barack_Obama.jpg (White House, 2012)
- `obama-2`: File:Official_portrait_of_Barack_Obama.jpg (2009)
- `biden-1`, `biden-3`: File:Joe_Biden_presidential_portrait.jpg (White House, 2021)
- `biden-2`: File:Biden_2013.jpg (White House, 2013)
- `harris-1`, `harris-3`: File:Kamala_Harris_Vice_Presidential_Portrait.jpg (White House, 2021)
- `harris-2`: File:Kamala_Harris_official_photo.jpg (US Senate, 2017)
